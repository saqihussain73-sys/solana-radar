Find recent public posts mentioning Solana tokens, independently of GMGN leaderboards. This is a mention detector, not proof of a recommendation or profitable call. Posts can be shilling, spam or coordinated promotion.

Choose a search and press Search Bluesky posts.

Sources and coverage

Bluesky: public post search, fetched when you press Search. Search coverage and browser access are not guaranteed.

X: opens X's latest-results search in a new tab. Automated X collection is not connected; no posts are imported or ranked from X.

Reddit / Telegram: not connected. Authentication, permissions or a suitable data service are needed before calling them live sources.

Contract detection recognises base58 strings 32–44 characters long. Such strings are only candidate Solana addresses, not validated token mints. The post's publication time is not the token's creation time. No automatic return, entry-price, wallet quality or scam score is inferred.
